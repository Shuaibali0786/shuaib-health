# API latency on the live API (T068), 2026-11-06

Read-only: 100 sequential GET requests in total (the agreed cap), no POST, no booking, no slots, from the owner's laptop with `backend/scripts/measure_latency.py` (about 16:57 UTC, 36 s in all). Target `https://shuaib-health-api.vercel.app`.

| Path | n | p50 | p95 | max | Status codes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/health` | 30 | 286 ms | **558 ms** | 741 ms | 200 x30 |
| `/api/v1/clinic` | 25 | 266 ms | 284 ms | 313 ms | 200 x19, **403 x6** |
| `/api/v1/doctors` | 25 | 254 ms | 273 ms | 305 ms | **403 x25** |
| `/api/v1/lab-tests` | 20 | 252 ms | 264 ms | 296 ms | **403 x20** |

## Result of the first run: PARTIAL (superseded by the spaced re-run below)
- `/health`: 30 good samples, p95 558 ms, under 1 s. **PASS** for that endpoint.
- Catalog: only the first 19 `/api/v1/clinic` requests returned 200 (those 19 were all under about 313 ms). **From the 20th request of the run on, every catalog request returned 403**, so the doctors and lab-tests timings are of the 403 responses, not of catalog work. They must **not** be read as catalog p95.
- Targets for SC-003 (p95 < 1 s) therefore remain **open for the catalog**.

## What the 403 was (found 6 Nov 2026 by the owner in the Vercel Firewall)
Vercel's automatic **"DDoS Mitigation" system rule challenged 47 requests** from the test IP between 21:58 and 22:08 PKT on 6 Nov. **Nothing was denied**, there are no custom rules, and Bot Protection is off. So the 403s came from the platform challenging a burst of about 100 requests in 36 s from one IP, not from the application. The hypothesis below was right.

## Earlier hypothesis (kept for the record)
- The application returns 403 only for a missing or wrong `X-Proxy-Secret` on booking writes and for admin rules (`backend/app/deps.py`, `errors.py`); the public catalog GET routes do not require the secret, and the app's own rate limiter answers 429, not 403.
- So the 403 most likely came from outside the app (a Vercel platform protection reacting to about 20 quick requests from one IP, or similar). **This is a hypothesis, not a finding.** It was not investigated further because the request budget was used up and the agent session has no Vercel team access.
- Why it matters: if a platform rule can block a single visitor after about 20 fast requests, it could also block a real visitor. The owner should look at Vercel -> `shuaib-health-api` -> Firewall / Observability for a denied-requests entry around 16:57 UTC on 6 Nov.

## Spaced re-run, 6 Nov 2026 (about 17:57 to 17:59 UTC, after the website redeploy)
Read-only, 60 sequential GETs, **one request every 2 seconds** (the agreed limit: at most 1 per 2 s, at most 60), no POST, no slots. `https://shuaib-health-api.vercel.app`, measured with curl total time from the owner's laptop. Order: /health, clinic, doctors, lab-tests, repeated 15 times.

| Path | n | p50 | p95 | max | Status codes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/health` | 15 | 310 ms | **345 ms** | 345 ms | 200 x15 |
| `/api/v1/clinic` | 15 | 289 ms | **647 ms** | 647 ms | 200 x15 |
| `/api/v1/doctors` | 15 | 296 ms | **354 ms** | 354 ms | 200 x15 |
| `/api/v1/lab-tests` | 15 | 290 ms | **315 ms** | 315 ms | 200 x15 |

**All 60 returned 200; no challenge, no 403. Every p95 is under 650 ms, against the target of 1 s (SC-003): PASS** for the endpoints measured. Caveats: n = 15 per path, so a p95 is effectively the slowest sample; the API was already warm (the first request of a cold function takes 4 to 6 s, see `cold-start.md`); `/slots` was not measured. The original plan of 200 catalog + 20 slot requests is not met, by the owner's cap.

## Lesson for future load tests
Vercel's DDoS Mitigation can challenge a single IP after a burst of roughly 20 or more requests in seconds. Space requests (at least 2 s apart) and keep totals small. `backend/scripts/measure_latency.py` has no delay option; a `--delay` flag would make this safe by default (not added).
